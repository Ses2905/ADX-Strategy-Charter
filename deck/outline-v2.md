=== SLIDE 19 (slide19.xml) ===
PLATFORM AUDIT FINDINGS
Advertisers Should Not Have to Translate Walmart to Use Walmart
Ad Center presents channels, buying methods, statuses, service models and internal systems as equivalent choices. The experience should begin with the media advertisers intend to buy, then introduce mechanics, setup requirements and technology only when relevant. Language is part of the product: unclear terminology adds friction, increases effort and weakens confidence in the platform.
ALL CAMPAIGNS
Buying Model
Use Display Auction for self-service campaigns. If auction and reserved inventory are both available, introduce the buying method during setup, where bidding, pricing and scheduling inputs differ.
Status vs. Media
Keep campaign status separate from media type. Move Archived into the Status filter and apply one consistent status model across campaigns and  reporting.
Media Scope
Treat Social and offsite media as distinct channels with different inventory, buyers and  funding - not as extensions of Onsite Display. Connect them through shared navigation and reporting.
CAMPAIGN GROUPS
Abbreviations
Replace DSS, MS and other platform-specific abbreviations with clear customer-facing names that do not require knowledge of Walmart’s internal language.
Metadata-Only
Prioritize customer-recognizable names over system-generated values. Surface IDs, platform codes and other technical metadata only when they support search, troubleshooting or reporting.
Channel Definitions
Reserve Channel for where media runs. Move DSPs, ad servers and other activation technology into separate platform metadata, i.e. The Trade Desk is a DSP, 1P DSP is an Ad-Server, not channel.
REPORTING
Product vs. Platform
Do not combine an inventory environment with a buying platform. Organize reporting around Onsite Display and Offsite Media – “DSP” not aligned with industry definition, ad center is also a DSP.
Ad Solutions’ Role
Clarify Walmart’s role by media type: inventory owner for onsite placements and audience, activation and measurement partner across most offsite inventory.
Reporting Context
Show total performance alongside channel-level contribution, and clearly identify differences in attribution, metric definitions and data freshness.
[NOTES] Advertisers should not have to translate Walmart to use Walmart. Terminology, taxonomy, and product organization should reflect how advertisers understand media buying — not internal ownership. | Ad Center presents channels, buying methods, statuses, service models and internal systems as equivalent choices. Language is part of the product: unclear terminology adds friction, increases effort and weakens confidence. | 19

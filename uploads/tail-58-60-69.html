Advertisers can’t be reduced to a single persona

Seven dimensions the platform should read and adapt to. See the detailed end-user maps that follow.

DimensionWhy it mattersExample signalsAdaptive experience
Customer segmentWho is the customer?Different segments have different business models, budgets, goals and success metrics.Enterprise brand · Mid-market brand · Agency · 3P seller · Non-endemic brand · Internal teamA 3P seller needs a simpler, more guided experience than an enterprise brand.
Service modelHow do they activate?Self-serve, managed, hybrid and API customers require fundamentally different experiences.Self-serve · Managed service · Hybrid · 3P API partner · Partner integrationSelf-serve customers need autonomy; managed-service customers need collaboration.
User roleWho is using the platform?Buyers, executives, analysts and administrators have different responsibilities and information needs.Media buyer · Commerce manager · Analyst · Executive · Sales director · Account managerExecutives need strategic insight; media buyers need execution tools.
Business objectiveWhat’s their goal?Customers come to grow sales, launch products, improve ROAS or acquire customers — not to use features.Launch a new product · Increase ROAS · Grow category share · Acquire new customers · Expand into new marketsLaunching a product requires a different experience than improving ROAS.
Jobs to be doneWhat’s their current task?The platform should recognize the task at hand and guide the most efficient workflow.Build a media strategy · Plan / forecast · Activate a campaign · Measure · Optimize · Build QBR reportPlanning, launching, measuring and optimizing all require different workflows.
ContextWhat’s happening in real time?Experience level, urgency, seasonality and account health determine the right guidance at the right time.New user · Holiday campaign · Budget nearly exhausted · Performance declining · Executive business reviewA first-time user should have a different experience than a power user preparing for Black Friday.
Behavior + intentWhat will they need next?Historical behavior, workflow patterns and performance let the platform recommend, automate and reduce effort over time.Recommended budgets · Surface performance · Audience generation · Draft campaign · Automate optimizationsAfter a Sponsored Search launch, recommend complementary activations and schedule the next review.
